Privacy Policy Last updated: September 25, 2026 BM Plumbing & Construction Inc. ("we", "us", "our") operates a private, internal job-history search tool used only by our own office staff. This policy explains how that tool handles data. What data we use The tool reads job and invoice records already stored in our Housecall Pro and QuickBooks Online accounts (job descriptions, addresses, customer names, invoice numbers, and related billing details) so our staff can search our own job history internally. Where the data lives Data is stored locally on our own office server and is not exposed to the public internet. It is accessible only within our office network. Third parties We use Housecall Pro and QuickBooks Online as the source systems for this data, under our existing accounts with each. We use Anthropic's Claude API to help refine some search results (for example, distinguishing a unit number from an unrelated number in a job description); short snippets of job description text may be sent to Anthropic for this purpose. We do not sell, rent, or share this data with any other third party. Who this applies to This tool is for internal use by BM Plumbing & Construction Inc. employees only. It is not a public-facing product and is not offered to other businesses or the general public. Contact Questions about this policy can be sent to andrey@bmplumbing.com.
